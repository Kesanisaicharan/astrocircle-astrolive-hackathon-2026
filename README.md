# ✦ AstroCircle — AstroLive Hackathon 2026

> **Astrology that becomes more engaging when you share it.**

AstroCircle is a product concept and working web prototype designed for the **AstroHack 2026 — Build the Next Universe** challenge. It explores how AstroLive can move beyond a consultation-only experience and create a stronger loop around **organic growth, habit formation, social engagement, and new revenue**.

## 🚀 Product Idea

AstroCircle combines:

- ✨ Personalized daily astrology
- 🔥 Daily streaks and lightweight rewards
- 👥 Friend-to-friend compatibility
- 📤 Shareable AstroCards
- 🔮 Expert astrologer discovery
- 👑 Premium 30-day astrology reports

### The growth loop

```text
Daily Reading
     ↓
Compatibility Result
     ↓
Share AstroCard
     ↓
Friend Joins
     ↓
Friend Gets Their Reading
     ↓
More Connections
     ↓
Return Tomorrow
```

The key product hypothesis is that a compatibility result is more naturally shareable than a generic referral request because the recipient becomes part of the experience.

## 🖥️ Prototype Experience

The current prototype demonstrates the core user journey:

1. **Landing / onboarding** — introduce AstroCircle and personalize the experience.
2. **Daily dashboard** — energy, love, career and finance guidance.
3. **AstroCircle** — discover compatibility with friends.
4. **Share flow** — turn a compatibility result into a social invitation.
5. **Astrologers** — discover available experts and start a chat.
6. **Premium report** — introduce a low-friction paid digital product.
7. **Profile** — track streaks, connections and progress.

> **Prototype note:** Prediction scores and copy are illustrative demo content. This MVP focuses on product UX and business mechanics, not production astrology calculations.
